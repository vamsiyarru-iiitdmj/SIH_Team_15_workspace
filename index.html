The gap on the ground

Advisory arrives too late

By the time a pest alert reaches a village, the window to act has often closed.

Data stays in silos

Soil health cards, weather feeds and mandi prices live in separate systems no farmer has time to cross-check.

One-size advice doesn't fit

A 2-acre plot in Malwa and a 20-acre plot in Punjab need different answers to the same question.

The platform

One field. One number to call.

Soil

Sensor + card fusion

Combines IoT soil-moisture readings with government soil health cards to recommend exact fertiliser dosage.

Sky

Satellite crop stress map

Weekly NDVI imagery flags stressed patches before they're visible to the eye.

Voice

IVR & WhatsApp advisory

No app required — advisories are read aloud in the farmer's dialect over a missed-call callback.

Mandi

Sell-time prediction

Forecasts a 7-day price window across nearby mandis so farmers time the harvest sale.
